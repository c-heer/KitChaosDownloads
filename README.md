# Kit Chaos

Your gear and packing lists, in one library that stays with you.

Download the newest version for the Mac under [Releases](https://github.com/c-heer/KitChaosDownloads/releases/latest).

- For Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13 Ventura or newer.
- The app isn't signed yet. On the first start macOS refuses to open it: open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. After that it opens like any other app.
- Your library stays in a folder you choose, like iCloud Drive or Dropbox, or on your Mac. Updating the app never touches it.
